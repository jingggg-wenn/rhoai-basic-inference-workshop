[#access-project]
= Access {productname-short} and Your Project

In this module, you will log in to the {productname-long} dashboard and access your pre-created Data Science project, which serves as your workspace for deploying models and running experiments.

== Step 2.1: Log In to {productname-short}

. Open your web browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended).
. Navigate to the *{productname-short} Dashboard URL* provided by your instructor.
. You will be redirected to the SSO login page.
+
image::ocp-rhoai-portal/img-login.png[SSO login page showing username and password fields,600]
. Enter your assigned username (e.g. `user1`) and password.
. Click *Sign In*.

You should now see the *OpenShift AI Dashboard* with a left sidebar showing menu items such as Projects, AI hub, Gen AI studio, and more.

image::ocp-rhoai-portal/img-rhoai-portal.png[OpenShift AI Dashboard]

image::ocp-rhoai-portal/img-access-ocp.png[Switching between OpenShift AI and OpenShift Console]
====

== Step 2.2: Navigate into Your Project

Your project has already been created for you. It follows the same naming convention as your username. For example, if your username is `user1`, your project is also named `user1`.

. Click *Projects* in the left sidebar.
+
image::ocp-rhoai-portal/img-project-list.png[Projects page listing pre-created workshop projects]
. Find the project that matches your username.
. Click into it.

You should now see your project with tabs for Overview, Workbenches, Pipelines, Deployments, Connections, and more.
